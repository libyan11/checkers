# Checkers

## ▶ [Play the game](https://libyan11.github.io/checkers/)

https://libyan11.github.io/checkers/

Checkers in your browser, on phone or computer. Play online with a friend, or against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard. Part of [Game Night](https://libyan11.github.io/).

**Play with a friend:** one person taps *Create a room* and shares the 4-letter code or link. The other types the code and taps *Join room*.

**Rules**
- Pieces move one square diagonally forward on the dark squares.
- Jump over an opponent's piece to capture it. Captures are compulsory, and a piece keeps jumping while it can.
- Reach the far side to be crowned king; kings move and capture backwards too. Getting crowned ends the move.
- Take all the opponent's pieces or leave them with no move to win.
- 40 moves each with only kings moving and no capture is a draw.
